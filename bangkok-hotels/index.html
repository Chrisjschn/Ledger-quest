Four credits, five nights

Each Platinum card account earns up to $300 per half-year (Jan–Jun and Jul–Dec) on prepaid “Pay Now” FHR and Hotel Collection bookings. The half-year is decided by the charge date, not the stay, so a May 2027 trip can use both the Jul–Dec 2026 and Jan–Jun 2027 credits. Hotel Collection bookings need 2+ nights; a credit can be spread over several bookings until it is used up.

Import prices
